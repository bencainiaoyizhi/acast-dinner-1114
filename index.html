Our Mission
我们的宗旨
◆

汇聚南湖及周边地区的华人企业家与专业人士，以交流学习为本，相互扶持，并为下一代搭建成长与实践的平台。我们秉持务实、可靠、以家庭为本的理念，以学习与交流、而非商业推广为宗旨。

A-CAST brings together Chinese-American entrepreneurs and professionals of the Southlake area to learn from one another, support one another, and build a platform for the next generation. Grounded, trustworthy, and family-centered, the Club is dedicated to learning and connection.
